# Saffron Retail Group — Online Store Policies

Applies to orders placed on the Saffron Retail Group online store (saffronretail.example). Policy version effective **1 October 2026**. Section numbers (for example **1.2**) are the citation IDs.

## 1. Returns and Refunds

### 1.1 Return window
Most products can be returned within **30 days of delivery**. The 30 days are counted from the delivery date shown in your order details. Exceptions are listed in Section 6.

### 1.2 Free returns, no restocking fee
Returns are **free**. We arrange a doorstep pickup at no cost and we do **not** charge any restocking fee. You do not need to ship the item back yourself.

### 1.3 Condition of returned items
Returned items must be unused and unwashed, with original tags, labels, packaging and any accessories or free gifts that came with them. Footwear must be returned in its original shoebox.

### 1.4 How to start a return
Go to **My Orders**, choose the item and select a return reason. A pickup is usually scheduled within 2 business days.

### 1.5 Refund timeline and method
The refund is initiated after the returned item passes a quality check, usually within 2 business days of pickup. Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method; the money usually reflects within 5–7 business days. Cash on Delivery (COD) orders are refunded to a bank account or UPI ID that you provide, or as Saffron store credit if you prefer.

### 1.6 Exchanges
Apparel and footwear can be exchanged for a different size or colour of the same product within the same 30-day window, subject to stock. If the size or colour you want is not available, you get a full refund instead.

### 1.7 Damaged, defective or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, raise a return within the 30-day window. We will replace it (subject to stock) or give a full refund. We may ask for photos.

## 2. Shipping and Delivery

### 2.1 Delivery charges
Delivery is **free on orders of ₹499 or more**. Orders below ₹499 have a delivery charge of **₹49**.

### 2.2 Delivery timelines
Metro cities: usually 2–4 business days after the order is placed. Other locations: usually 4–7 business days. The estimated delivery date is shown at checkout.

### 2.3 Where we deliver
We deliver **only within India**, to serviceable PIN codes (checked at checkout). We do not ship internationally.

### 2.4 Order tracking
A tracking link is sent by SMS and email once your order is dispatched.

### 2.5 Stock status
Products marked **out of stock** cannot be ordered; we do not take pre-orders or back-orders. **Low stock** means only a few units are left. Stock shown is as of the catalogue snapshot.

## 3. Warranty

### 3.1 Which products carry a warranty
A product carries a brand warranty only if its catalogue entry lists a warranty period (`warranty_months`). The warranty runs for that many months from the date of delivery. This covers most small appliances, electronics accessories and personal-care devices (trimmers, hair dryers, hair straighteners), and some cookware, luggage and calculators. If no warranty period is listed, the product has no brand warranty, but it is still covered by our 30-day return policy (Section 1).

### 3.2 What the warranty covers
Manufacturing defects that appear under normal household use. The brand's service partner will repair the product, or replace it if a repair is not possible.

### 3.3 What the warranty does not cover
Physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, unauthorised repairs, normal wear and tear, and consumable parts such as gaskets, filters and batteries.

### 3.4 How to make a warranty claim
Contact Saffron customer support (Section 8) with your order number and a short description or photos of the problem. We coordinate with the brand's service partner. Keep the invoice that was emailed to you.

## 4. Payments

### 4.1 Accepted payment methods
UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, popular mobile wallets and Cash on Delivery (COD).

### 4.2 Cash on Delivery
COD is available for orders up to **₹10,000** in total value, at serviceable PIN codes. There is **no extra fee** for COD. You can pay the delivery partner in cash or by UPI.

### 4.3 Failed payments
If money is debited but the order is not placed, it is reversed automatically to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 business days.

## 5. Cancellations

### 5.1 Before dispatch
You can cancel an order free of charge any time **before it is dispatched**, from My Orders. Prepaid orders get a full refund as per Section 1.5.

### 5.2 After dispatch
Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery, or return the item after delivery as per Section 1.

## 6. Non-returnable Items

### 6.1 Beauty and personal care products
For hygiene reasons, beauty and personal care products (face wash, shampoo, hair oil, body lotion, sunscreen, lip balm, deodorants and perfumes) **cannot be returned once the seal is opened**. Unopened, sealed items can be returned within 30 days. Damaged, defective or wrong items are always covered by Section 1.7.

### 6.2 In-ear audio products
Wired earphones and wireless earbuds cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken, unless the item is damaged, defective or wrong (Section 1.7).

### 6.3 Personal grooming devices
Beard trimmers cannot be returned once used, unless the item is damaged, defective or wrong (Section 1.7).

## 7. Prices and Offers

### 7.1 Selling price and MRP
The price shown is the **selling price**, inclusive of GST. The MRP is shown for reference; the discount is the difference between the MRP and the selling price.

### 7.2 Price changes
Prices can change. The price at the moment you place the order is the price you pay. Prices in the catalogue are current as of the snapshot date, 1 October 2026.

### 7.3 Coupons
Only one coupon can be used per order. Coupons cannot be combined and cannot be applied to delivery charges.

### 7.4 No price matching
We do not match prices from other websites or stores.

### 7.5 Sale and discounted items
Items bought at a discount or in a sale have the same return policy (Section 1) and warranty (Section 3) as full-price items.
